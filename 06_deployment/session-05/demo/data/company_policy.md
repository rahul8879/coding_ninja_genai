# Acme Technologies Employee Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave should normally be requested at least 7 calendar days in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate may be requested when sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive working days.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week subject to manager approval and project requirements.

## Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for approved courses, certifications, conferences, and technical books.

## Relocation
Relocation support is evaluated case by case. The policy does not define a guaranteed relocation allowance amount.

## Data Security
Confidential company or client data must not be uploaded to public AI systems unless explicitly approved. API keys, passwords, and credentials must never be committed to source control.
