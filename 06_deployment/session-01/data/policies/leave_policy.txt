ACME INDIA — LEAVE POLICY (effective 1 April 2026)

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is credited monthly and requires manager approval. Up to 5 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year; the remainder expires on 31 March. Employees also receive 10 days of paid sick leave. Planned leave longer than three working days should be requested at least two weeks in advance.
