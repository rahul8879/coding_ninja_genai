ACME INDIA — BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY (effective 1 April 2026)

Employees travelling within India may claim hotel expenses up to INR 6,000 per night in tier-1 cities and INR 4,000 per night in other cities. Economy airfare is permitted for domestic travel. International travel requires written approval from a business-unit leader before booking. Receipts are mandatory for individual expenses above INR 500.
