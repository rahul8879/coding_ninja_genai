ACME INDIA — EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY (effective 1 April 2026)

Business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction. The claim must include a business purpose and required receipts. Approved claims are normally paid in the next payroll cycle. Personal purchases, traffic fines, alcohol, and expenses without a business purpose are not reimbursable. This policy does not define an employee-relocation benefit.
